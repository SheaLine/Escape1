#Escape1

How to Escape:
1. Spawn in cell
2. Pick up dog treats in the cell
3. Go to the cell door and wave the dog treat in front of the dog
4. The dog comes over and lets you out of the cell (he now follows you for the rest of the
game)
5. Avoid guards and climb up the ladder
6. Talk to prisoner NPC in the first hallway on the left (he tells you about the keycard locked
behind bars above)
7. Go upstairs and all the way to the right, down the ladder, to get to the computer room,
avoiding guards along the way
8. To open the bars to get the keycard, interact with the computer, and complete the
mini-game
9. Go back to the keycard room to pick up the key, once the key is picked up the door
inside will open and lead directly upward to the next stage
